# OSINT Mastery — A Module-Based Learning Repository

> Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) is the practice of collecting and analyzing publicly
> available information to produce actionable intelligence. This repo is a structured,
> student-friendly curriculum for learning OSINT the **right** way — legally, ethically,
> and effectively.

## Who this is for
Cybersecurity students, aspiring penetration testers, SOC analysts, journalists,
researchers, and anyone who wants to understand how digital footprints are built —
and how to protect their own.

## ⚠️ Ground Rules Before You Start
1. **Only investigate targets you own, that you have explicit written permission to
   test, or that are part of a public-interest research context (journalism, academic
   research) with proper legal review.**
2. Never use OSINT findings to harass, dox, blackmail, stalk, or gain unauthorized
   access to accounts/systems. That is no longer OSINT — it is a crime.
3. "Public" does not mean "fair game for any use." Aggregating scattered public data
   into a profile on a real person, without consent, can itself be illegal (stalking /
   harassment law) even if every individual fact was technically public.
4. Document your scope and intent before every practical exercise.
