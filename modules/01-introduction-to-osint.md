# Module 01 — Introduction to OSINT

## What is OSINT?
**OSINT (Open Source Intelligence)** is intelligence gathered from publicly available
sources — no hacking, no brute force, no unauthorized access. Just structured,
smart searching of information anyone could legally find.

> "OSINT isn't about *what* you can find — it's about *connecting* what's already there."

## The OSINT Cycle
1. **Direction** — Define the objective and legal scope
2. **Collection** — Gather data from open sources
3. **Processing** — Organize raw data (spreadsheets, graphs, timelines)
4. **Analysis** — Connect data points into intelligence
5. **Dissemination** — Report findings to the relevant stakeholder
6. **Feedback** — Refine scope based on findings

## Real-World Users of OSINT
| Who | Why |
|---|---|
| Ethical Hackers / Pentesters | Recon before authorized engagements |
| Law Enforcement | Missing persons, cybercrime investigation |
| Journalists | Fact-checking, exposing disinformation |
| CTF Players | Capture-the-flag OSINT challenges |
| Threat Intel Analysts | Tracking APT infrastructure |
| Recruiters / Due Diligence | Background verification |
| Everyday People | Checking their own digital exposure |

## The OSINT Framework (Categories)
- Search Engines & Dorking
- Social Media Intelligence (SOCMINT)
- Image & Video Intelligence (IMINT)
- Geolocation Intelligence (GEOINT)
- Domain / Infrastructure Intelligence
- People Search / Username & Email Intelligence
- Dark Web Intelligence (with extreme caution & legal review)

## Legal vs Illegal — First Look
| Legal (OSINT) | Illegal (Not OSINT) |
|---|---|
| Google dorking public files | Exploiting a login form |
| Reading public social posts | Breaking into a private account |
| WHOIS lookup | Installing spyware/keyloggers |
| Reverse image search | Phishing links to steal credentials |
| Public breach-check tools | Buying/selling breach data |

See **[Module 09](09-legal-ethics-and-boundaries.md)** for the full legal deep-dive.

## Case Studies Referenced Throughout This Course
- **Bellingcat** — MH17, Skripal poisoning, Syrian chemical attacks, Navalny

  leaked passport databases, metadata, geolocation).

## Practice Exercise (Safe, Legal)
Google dork your own name/email in quotes and see what's public about *you*.
This teaches the technique without touching anyone else's privacy.
